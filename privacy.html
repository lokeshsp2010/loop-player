Privacy

Loop has no accounts, no server and no analytics. The developer collects no data about you.

Your queue, repeat setting, Jamendo client ID and any songs you choose to keep offline are stored only in your own browser. Clear your browser's site data to remove them.

When you play a YouTube link, YouTube's embedded player loads and YouTube may collect data under the Google Privacy Policy. By using Loop's YouTube feature you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. When you search free music, your search words and client ID are sent to Jamendo.

Songs you add from your own device never leave your device.
